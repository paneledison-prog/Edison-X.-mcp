Day-by-day timeline of bookings and activities beside a map of the route.

## Structure

- **Layout:** Left itinerary with day sections and timed cards; right map; header with trip dates and travelers.
- **Components:** day section, booking card, timeline rail, route map, add activity button
- **Typography:** 15px card text, 20px day titles.
- **Color usage:** White and pale cyan surfaces, teal accents, orange for pending items.
- **Density:** balanced

## Build hints

- Show travel time between stops
- Allow drag reordering within a day
- Mark unconfirmed bookings clearly
