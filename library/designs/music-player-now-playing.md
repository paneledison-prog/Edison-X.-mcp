Large artwork, scrubber, transport controls and a pull-up queue.

## Structure

- **Layout:** Vertical stack: header, square artwork, title block, scrubber, controls row, secondary actions; queue sheet from the bottom.
- **Components:** artwork, scrubber, transport controls, queue sheet, like button
- **Typography:** Bold 24px track title, 14px artist, tabular time labels.
- **Color usage:** Dark base with a background tint sampled from the artwork and one green accent.
- **Density:** airy

## Build hints

- Sample the artwork for a gradient background
- Keep the play button the largest control
- Support swipe down to dismiss
