Left half shows a brand panel with a quote, right half shows email and password fields plus social sign-in.

## Structure

- **Layout:** Two equal columns; form vertically centered at 380px max width; brand panel full height with a tinted background.
- **Components:** brand panel, email field, password field with reveal, social button row, text link
- **Typography:** 16px labels above inputs, 28px form title.
- **Color usage:** Indigo brand panel, white form side, red only for error text.
- **Density:** airy
