- **Platform:** android
- **Tags:** health-fitness, mobile, calendar, progress-tracker, playful
- **Palette:** #fef3c7, #ffffff, #f59e0b, #7c3aed, #1f2937

Daily habit checklist with streak flames and a monthly heat calendar.

## Structure

- **Layout:** Header with date strip; list of habit cards with check controls; monthly heatmap section; floating add button.
- **Components:** date strip, habit card, streak badge, heatmap calendar, floating action button
- **Typography:** Rounded sans, 18px habit names, 12px streak counts.
- **Color usage:** Warm yellow background, amber and purple accents, white cards.
- **Density:** balanced

## Build hints

- Celebrate streaks with subtle motion
- Let users skip a day without breaking a streak
- Keep the add button reachable with one thumb
