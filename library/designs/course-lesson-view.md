- **Layout:** Left outline with checkmarks; central video; tabs for overview, notes and discussion; sticky next lesson button.
- **Components:** course outline, video player, progress bar, tab bar, next lesson button
- **Typography:** 16px body, 22px lesson title.
- **Color usage:** White and lavender surfaces, purple progress and actions.
- **Density:** balanced

## Build hints
