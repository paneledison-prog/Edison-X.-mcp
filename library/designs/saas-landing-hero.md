Centered headline with two calls to action above a large product screenshot, followed by logo strip and feature trio.

## Structure

- **Layout:** Sticky top navigation; centered hero; full-width screenshot with soft shadow; logo strip; three-column feature grid; footer.
- **Components:** sticky header, hero headline, primary and secondary buttons, logo strip, feature card
- **Typography:** Large 56px tight-leading headline, 18px muted subhead, 14px nav.
- **Color usage:** White canvas, one indigo accent on the primary button, slate for text.
- **Density:** airy

## Build hints

- Limit the hero to one primary action
- Round screenshot corners and add a faint gradient glow behind it
- Keep the feature grid to three items per row
