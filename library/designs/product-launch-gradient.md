# Product launch page with animated gradient

- **Category:** landing-page
- **Platform:** web
- **Tags:** colorful, futuristic, hero-section, glassmorphism, dark
- **Palette:** #0a0a1a, #6d28d9, #ec4899, #22d3ee, #f8fafc

Dark launch page with a slow mesh gradient behind frosted cards and an email capture form.

## Structure

- **Layout:** Full-viewport hero with centered content; frosted feature cards in a three-up row; sticky mini footer with the form.
- **Components:** gradient background, glass card, email capture form, countdown, badge
- **Typography:** Wide geometric sans, 64px headline, 16px body with generous tracking on labels.
- **Color usage:** Deep navy base, violet to pink gradient, cyan used for the submit button.
- **Density:** balanced

## Build hints

- Keep text on glass cards above 4.5:1 contrast
- Pause the gradient animation for reduced-motion users
- Place the form above the fold
