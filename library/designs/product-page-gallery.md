Image gallery on the left, a buy box that stays in view on the right, reviews and specs below.

## Structure

- **Layout:** Two columns: thumbnail strip plus large image; sticky purchase panel with price, options and add-to-bag; tabs for details.
- **Components:** thumbnail strip, option selector, add to bag button, rating summary, tab bar
- **Typography:** 14px body, 28px product title, 24px price.
- **Color usage:** White background, charcoal text, green used for stock status and the primary button.
- **Density:** balanced

## Build hints

- Keep the buy box sticky on desktop only
- Show stock and delivery estimate near the button
- Make the image zoom keyboard accessible
